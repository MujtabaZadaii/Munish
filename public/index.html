Data Import & Template Guide

1. Invoices Excel Import (.xlsx / .csv)

If you have past sales records or invoices in Excel, download the sample template below and organize your columns accordingly:

  • Invoice No (e.g. INV-1001) - For multi-item invoices, use the same Invoice No across rows. They will automatically be grouped into a single bill.
  • Date (e.g. 2026-11-20)
  • Customer Name & Phone (Customer's full name and mobile number)
  • Customer Address (Delivery address or city)
  • Item Name, Quantity, Price (Product description, quantity, and selling price)
  • Discount & Discount Type (Line discount amount or percentage)
  • Bill Discount, Charges, Received (Overall invoice discount, delivery charges, and cash received)
2. Products Catalog Import (.xlsx / .csv)

Bulk import your entire shop catalog and price list from an Excel sheet into MySQL in one click:

  • Product Name (Required: Item name, e.g. PVC Pipe 1 inch)
  • Selling Price (Required: Price per unit, e.g. 450)
  • Unit (Optional: Pcs, Kg, Box, Pkt, Dozen, Mtr)
  • Category (Optional: e.g. Sanitary, Hardware, Fittings)
